# Homeowners Insurance Research — Montclair, Oakland (94611/94605, VHFHSZ)

_Research compiled from a deep-research workflow + targeted supplementary searches, late 2026. Sources cited inline._

## TL;DR

1. **Check [Kin.com](https://www.kin.com) first** — instant address-level quote, writes in every CA wildfire zone. Then exhaust the admitted market — it is dramatically cheaper when available (~$2,200/yr vs $7–12K/yr FAIR stack).
2. Best admitted bets today: **Mercury, Farmers, CSAA/AAA, Travelers** (all committed to grow in fire zones under CA's Sustainable Insurance Strategy).
3. **Delos Insurance** (SF-based MGA with parcel-level wildfire modeling) actively writes Montclair-type homes other carriers decline — often the best "good rate in a fire zone" option. Broker-only, <5 min per address.
4. Fallback: **FAIR Plan + DIC wrap** ≈ $7,000–12,000/yr for a $1.5M Oakland hills home. 94611 average FAIR premium: $5,020 (June 2026), ~$6,481 after the 29.1% increase effective Oct 15, 2026.
5. Home hardening (Class A roof, ember vents, Zone Zero) is now the **entry ticket** for admitted carriers, not just a discount.

## Delos Insurance — the fire-zone specialist

- SF-based **MGA** (not a carrier) using parcel-level wildfire modeling (satellite, wind data, suppression efficacy). Says 65% of homes other insurers deem too risky are acceptable to its model; >50% of FAIR Plan homes eligible for a single comprehensive Delos policy.
- Eligible for ~12M CA homes after Mar 2026 expansion. Distributed via 14,000 CA brokers/agents.
- **Ask your broker: "whose paper?"** — policies are issued by **Homesite (admitted, CIGA-backed)** or **Lloyd's of London (surplus lines, no guaranty fund, +3% surplus tax + stamping fee)**.
- Claims of zero fire losses in Jan 2025 LA fires are company press-release claims, not audited.
- Sources: [PRNewswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/delos-expands-insurance-eligibility-by-more-than-1-million-homes-in-wildfire-stressed-areas-302722183.html), [Menlo Insurance guide](https://www.menloinsurance.com/library/guides/delos-insurance-california)

## Actual pricing — Montclair / Oakland hills

**FAIR Plan ZIP-level data (June 2026, [Houseberry](https://www.houseberry.com/blog/fair-plan-rate-increase-bay-area-hills-zip-codes/)):**

- **94611 (Oakland hills/Montclair): 2,822 FAIR Plan homes, avg premium $5,020/yr → ~$6,481 after +29.1% (effective Oct 15, 2026)**

- **Wood shake roof = near-uninsurable.** Replace first.

## FAIR Plan + DIC mechanics

- FAIR Plan covers fire/lightning/internal explosion/smoke only — **no liability, theft, water**. Residential cap **$3M** ($20M is commercial only).
- DIC wrap restores HO-3-like coverage. DIC writers per CDI: **Mercury, CSAA, Farmers, Nationwide, Travelers (Standard Fire), Zurich, Foremost**. ([CDI DIC list](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/5-residential/carriersDICpolicies.cfm))
- FAIR Plan enrollment: ~666K residential policies (Jun 2026) — tripled since 2021.
- 29.1% avg rate increase effective Oct 15, 2026 (requested 35.8%, approved lower).
- $1B post-LA-fires assessment (Feb 2025) may surface as a temporary supplemental fee on all CA policyholders.

## Discounts & hardening

- **Safer from Wildfires** (mandated since 2022): FAIR Plan offers up to 12 mitigation discounts; qualifying for all = up to **16.4% off the wildfire portion**. Keep receipts/photos; inspection may be required.
- **IBHS Wildfire Prepared Home** designation (Essential/Enhanced) — state-endorsed, increasingly gates admitted eligibility.
- Mercury's minimums (above) are a good checklist for what any admitted carrier will want.

## Checking availability online by address

Three tiers, ranked by usefulness:

### True address-level, instant & self-serve

- **Kin — [kin.com](https://www.kin.com)** — best first stop. Direct-to-consumer, fully digital quoting by address; explicitly writes in **all 58 CA counties including every wildfire zone** (expanded in CA after the Jan 2025 fires, never retreated). Quote flow pre-fills most property data → real number in minutes. Company example: hardening features (ember guards, concrete tile roof, defensible space) cut one premium from $6K → $2K/yr. Also added condo + flood options (Jul 2026).
- **Mercury — [mercuryinsurance.com](https://www.mercuryinsurance.com)** — online quote by address; in VHFHSZ may route to an agent rather than binding online, but the admitted carrier most worth trying given its fire-zone growth commitment.
- **Stand — [stand.insure](https://www.stand.insure)** — only for homes **$2M–$10M** (a typical ~$1.6M Montclair home falls below its minimum). Builds a physics "digital twin" of the address; returns a price + prioritized mitigation plan. Reference: ~$12–15K/yr for a $3M high-fire-risk home. Partnered with Frontline Wildfire Defense (May 2026): exterior defense systems can cut modeled risk up to 60% → premium reduction.

### ZIP-level (build a call list, not availability)

- **CDI Home Insurance Finder — [insurance.ca.gov](https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/107-top10guides/home-insurance-finder.cfm)** — official state directory. Choose Homeowners/Dwelling Fire, enter 94611, check the **"higher fire risk areas"** box. Lists companies + local agents, but listed ≠ accepting new business.
- **CAL FIRE FHSZ map — [fire.ca.gov](https://fire.ca.gov/what-we-do/fire-resource-assessment-program/fire-hazard-severity-zones)** — verify the parcel's exact fire-zone designation (context, plus State Farm's planned re-entry requires a wildfire-safety designation).

### Address-level but broker-only

- **Delos** — no consumer-facing checker; a broker enters the address in Delos' portal and gets an answer in <5 minutes (~14,000 appointed CA brokers/agents).
- **California FAIR Plan — [cfpnet.com](https://www.cfpnet.com)** — online premium *calculator* available, but actual applications must go through a licensed agent/broker.

Caveat: an online decline isn't always final — agent/broker channels often have broader underwriting appetite than the consumer website for the same carrier.

## Action plan for a Montclair purchase

1. Gather facts once: year built, roof age/material, sqft, rebuild estimate, claims history, hardening features.
2. **Kin.com first** — instant yes/no + real premium at the specific address, zero friction.
3. **Mercury online quote** — potential admitted-market rate (may route to an agent in VHFHSZ).
4. Run the **CDI Home Insurance Finder** for 94611 (check the "higher fire risk" box) → pick 2–3 **independent brokers** who work Oakland hills fire zones. Have them check **Delos** (<5 min per address), other admitted carriers (**Farmers, CSAA, Travelers, Liberty Mutual**), and price a **FAIR+DIC** stack as the floor.
5. Compare total annual cost (premium + surplus taxes/fees + wildfire deductible), rebuild amount, and whether paper is admitted (CIGA-backed) vs surplus.
6. Budget: **$6–12K/yr** insurance for a ~$1.5–1.6M Montclair home unless an admitted carrier accepts it (then possibly $2.5–6K). On a purchase, that's ~$500–1,000/month of effective ownership cost vs a flatland home.
7. If hardening is incomplete, price out Class A roof/ember vents/Zone Zero — it unlocks admitted-market eligibility and mandated discounts, and State Farm's planned re-entry requires a wildfire-safety designation.
