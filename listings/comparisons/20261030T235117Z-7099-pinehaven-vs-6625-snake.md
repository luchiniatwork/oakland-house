## Verdict

**Pick: 7099-pinehaven**, for the privacy- and value-minded posture — a solo buyer who works from home and wants a guest/rental wing. The single reason it wins: it matches Snake on the top-weighted view and on layout/condition while beating it on kitchen, privacy, primary suite, outdoor space, and lot usability — at $361/sf vs $561.50/sf — and it carries none of the three structural red flags the buyer named (flat roofs, stilt foundations, power lines) that Snake stacks in one property. Snake is the better *house-as-object* (light, MCM purity, solar, AC); Pinehaven is the better *buy*.
