## 3. Verdict and sensitivity

**Overall pick: 6401 Snake Rd**, for the view-first posture this buyer profile
encodes. It wins for one reason: it is the only house of the pair that delivers a
real, living-level panoramic view — the single highest-weighted dimension — while
also leading on privacy, primary suite, and outdoor space, and it does so $101K
cheaper. The practical caveat is that it is **Pending**: pursue as a backup-offer
candidate and treat its score as the benchmark any Active listing (including
Wilton) must beat.

**Sensitivity:**

1. **Availability flip (the real one).** The ranking is robust in score space —
   even zeroing the view dimension entirely and renormalizing, Snake still leads
   (3.96 vs 3.74), because it also wins privacy, primary suite, and outdoor space.
   What actually flips this comparison is the status line: if "must be able to
   transact now" is a hard constraint, Snake's Pending status disqualifies it and
   Wilton wins by default. No plausible single-dimension *score* change flips the
   ranking — Snake stays ahead even if its view is re-scored 3.0 (→ 3.79) or its
   privacy 2.0 (→ 3.99); it takes roughly simultaneous downgrades of view → 2.5
   AND privacy → 2.5 AND primary_suite → 3.5 just to tie.
2. **Insurance-quote asymmetry.** FHSZ is a shared flag on paper, but the parity
   could break at quote time: Snake's wood siding + dry-grass understory and
   firestorm-footprint location may return materially worse quotes than Wilton's
   newer, rebuilt envelope (defensible-space work aside). If quotes diverge by
   several $K/yr, the $101K price advantage erodes fast — get both quotes before
   treating the FHSZ row as a true tie.
