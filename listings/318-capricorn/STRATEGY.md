Negotiation playbook for pursuing (or walking from) this agent-owned listing.
Companion files: `listing.json` (scored record, 3.8/5), `card.txt`,

`photos/`. Last updated: 2026-11-03.

**Core mental model:** an emotionally magnetic house (character, light,
staging) sitting on a risk stack (water-by-design garage, eroding landslide-zoned
slope, unpermitted work, fire-insurance exposure) sold by a **sophisticated
seller with ~zero equity room**. Her carryback floor is near ask; the market
wants to bid character houses up. Your job: quote insurance first, convert the
risk stack into price/credits gate by gate, and never pay character-money for
documented-but-unpriced defects.

## 1. Leverage inventory (who can push, and why)

**Your leverage:**
- **Carryback math:** $835K note + ~$50K sale costs ≈ **$885K floor** — she
  cannot take a real discount without bringing cash. If the note has a 5-year
  **balloon (~Jan 2026)**, she is under hard deadline pressure right now.
- **Vacant + already bought in Elmwood** = two housing payments + certainty
  preference. Time is her cost.
- **The package itself thins competition.** Flooding, erosion, rats,
  encroachments, noise, and theft are all disclosed in writing — many buyers
  will self-select out. The missing pest report reads like a red flag to any
  careful buyer's agent.
- **Documented disclosure misses:** CE2000130 vs TDS "No"; SPQ lead-reno "No"
  while a pre-1978 house was fully prepped/painted in 2026 (RRP). She is a
  licensee — these are professional exposure, and she knows it.
- **Unpermitted-work stack** (2026 kitchen/bath plumbing, water heater, AC, EV
  charger, studio power, retaining walls) — retro-permitting risk she carries
  until close.
- **Seller-side inspection is soft** (BPG: no action items, big exclusions) —
  independent findings will be new information, not re-litigation.
- **FHSZ "NonWildland" determination cuts both ways:** it cleans up her
  statutory disclosure, but it also means *her* documentation doesn't answer the
  insurance question at all — your insurance contingency is unimpeachable.

**Her leverage:**
- **DOM 5 in a teaser market:** hills comps went +26% (6401 Snake) to +86%
  (6833 Snake) over ask; 6625 Snake (3.4) listed the same day at $849K is the
  direct competitor. Character houses photograph bid-ups.
- **She is a pro:** no listing-side commission cost, no emotional fog, knows
  every form, and has pre-disclosed the worst of it (hard to claim concealment
  later on the disclosed items).
- **Her floor ≈ ask** means she may simply hold rather than discount — plan for
  a seller who would rather wait than go below payoff.

## 10. Offer sequence

**Pre-offer (free, 1–2 days):**
1. Call/text the agent (she picks up — it's her house): offer date or
   pre-emptives? Any offers in hand? Her Elmwood close timeline?
2. Ask for: **pest report**, **CE2000130 closure**, **note balance/balloon**
   (via escrow benefit statement), her **insurance carrier/premium**, offer
   date.
3. **Get the insurance quote started the same day.**
4. Optional but strong: request access for **pre-inspections** (vacant house;
   geotech + pest + drainage before offering = asymmetric information).

**Serve a written BMI** (C.A.R. Buyer Material Information request) with the
full demand list: `disclosure-analysis.md` §1–2 — pest report, CE file, permit
finals, special-inspection letters, warranties, Duval full billing, Jarvis
plans, note payoff, insurance history, appliance list, parking explanation,
furnace/WH dates.

**Offer structure:**
- **No competition signal:** open **$860–875K**, 21–25 day close (fast = the
  currency she values), full contingencies: inspection 14d, loan, appraisal,
  **insurance-bindability**, title/survey review, BMI documents due in 3 days.
- **Competition likely:** **$895K–930K** with shortened-but-alive contingencies
  (inspection 7–10d after pre-inspections, insurance contingency never waived
  on a hills house), escalation capped at **$950K** — above that, let a
  character-buyer own the risk stack.
- **Concession menu (trade, don't give):** price ↔ pest/WDO cure; price ↔
  drainage completion by Duval pre-close; price ↔ seismic strap package;
  price ↔ retro-permitting of 2026 work; warranties/plans transferred;
  encroachment agreements papered pre-close; close speed ↔ price.
- Earnest money: standard 3%; liquidated-damages cap acknowledged; no
  non-refundable anything until §9 gates 1–4 green.

**During contingency:** run §3–§7 in parallel; convert findings into written
credits **before** the clock dies; every credit documented as a dollar, not a
promise (exception: Alley Cat warranty transfer + pest Section 1 completion —
those close before close).

**Before removing contingencies:** §8 triggers all checked clear; insurance
bound in writing; payoff statement reconciled by escrow.
