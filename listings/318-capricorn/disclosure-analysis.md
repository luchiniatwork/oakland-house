## 6. Bottom line

The 11/4 re-export closed the package's two biggest documentary gaps: the **pest
report is now in** (no active termite infestation; priced Section I only $710;
but three Section I rot items sit unpriced as "BY OTHERS," earth-to-wood contact
is pervasive, and the roof/attic/upper eaves/rear structures went uninspected)
and the **french-drain as-built is now in** (protects the below-grade family
room; discharges onto the street; full billing still missing — one invoice is
still duplicated in the package). The package remains unusually honest in prose
(the seller-agent disclosed flooding, erosion, encroachments, rats, noise, and
theft — more than most), but it is still **document-thin where it counts**: a
code-enforcement abatement is contradicted by a "No" on the TDS, most 2024-2026
work is unpermitted, and the engineering story of the hillside (stabilization
