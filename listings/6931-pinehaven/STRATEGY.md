**Core mental model:** a short-sale offer buys an *option* on the house, not the
house. The seller can sign; only the lender(s) can sell. Size emotional and
financial commitment accordingly.

## 1. Short-sale process — how to tackle it

### Before offering (cheap, fast diligence)

- [ ] **Ask the listing agent (Cameron Platt, Vanguard, 510-682-3982):**
  - Is this an **approved** short sale (lender has already agreed to a price) or
    unapproved? Approved = weeks; unapproved = months.
  - Who is the servicer/lender? Is a short-sale negotiator assigned?
  - **How many liens?** Any HELOC, 2nd mortgage, tax or HOA liens? (Each junior
    lienholder must separately approve — the #1 late-stage deal killer.)
  - Has the seller submitted a **complete hardship package**? Incomplete packages
    stall files for months.
  - Why the Feb→Mar price *raise* to $1.4M and July withdrawal? (Tests whether the
    bank already rejected numbers once — tells you where the BPO anchored.)
- [ ] **Title/lien search early** (preliminary title report via buyer's agent or
  title co). Confirm lien stack before spending on inspections.
- [ ] **Estimate the shortfall.** If 2021 was 20% down, first lien ≈ $980K;
  $1.049M list nets ~$970K after ~7–8% costs → shortfall is *small* (easier
  approval) **or** there's undisclosed junior debt (harder). The answer to the
  lien question decides which world you're in.
- [ ] **Get an insurance quote NOW** (see §2) — before offering, not in escrow.

### Offer structure

- [ ] Use the **C.A.R. Short Sale Addendum (SSA)** with an explicit
  **lender-approval deadline** (e.g. 45–60 days) so you're not parked indefinitely.
- [ ] Keep **inspection and loan contingencies alive until after lender approval**,
  not from offer acceptance — otherwise contingency clocks burn while the bank sits.
- [ ] Offer at or near list with clean terms rather than lowballing — the bank
  counters on *its* BPO, not on your bid; a strong clean offer mainly beats
  competing buyers, not the bank.
- [ ] Earnest money: standard amount, confirm refundability through the approval
  period. It will be tied up for months — treat it as illiquid.
- [ ] **Do not rate-lock.** You can't hold a lock through an unbounded approval.
  Budget the payment at +0.5–0.75% above today's quote as stress case.

### During the wait

- [ ] Set a **personal walk-away date** (e.g. 75 days without approval) and honor it.
- [ ] Keep touring other listings. Recall > precision applies to you too — this
  option is not exclusive until the bank signs.
- [ ] Re-verify insurance quote every ~30 days; CA market is hardening mid-deal.
- [ ] If the bank counters above list: re-run the math against the $520/sf comp.
  Above ~$1.15M the discount thesis is dead — walk.

### Collapse scenarios (pre-decide the response)

| Scenario | Response |
|---|---|
| Bank counters > $1.15M | Walk; discount thesis dead vs comp |
| Junior lienholder surfaces | Demand seller/lienholders resolve; do not pay junior-lien shortfall yourself |
| Approval > 75 days | Walk per walk-away date |
| Foreclosure auction date set mid-process | Confirm postponement in writing or exit |
| Seller asked for contribution/note and balks | Deal likely dead; don't bridge it |

## 2. Fire / insurance (Very High FHSZ — gating item)

- [ ] **Bindability check before offer:** quote from at least 2 admitted carriers;
  expect decline → get **CA FAIR Plan + DIC wrap** quote as the real baseline.
  Budget: FAIR + DIC on a 2,336 sf hills home is typically several $K/yr — put the
  actual number in the affordability model, not a guess.
- [ ] Ask seller's agent for the **current insurance carrier and premium** (if any)
  and any prior claims (CLUE report via seller).
- [ ] **Defensible-space scope:** eucalyptus touching decks/structure (p_08–p_10,
  p_38–p_42, p_47) needs limb-up/removal quotes — get an arborist estimate during
  inspection period; it's both a safety and an insurability lever (some carriers
  require documented mitigation).
- [ ] Hardening checklist for later: ember-resistant vents, gutter guards, Class A
  roof (already pitched shingle — verify rating), deck-board/ledger condition,
  0–5 ft non-combustible zone around structure.
- [ ] Note for the file: power lines at street (p_01) — cosmetic/EMF preference
  item, not a deal issue.

## 3. Physical diligence (hillside 1978 structure)

Inspection budget: plan **$1.5–2.5K**, all sunk cost if the bank kills the deal.
Order matters — cheapest/most decisive first:

1. [ ] **General home inspection** (whole-house).
2. [ ] **Foundation/drainage** — downslope 3-level structure with cantilevered
   decks; ask specifically about piers/posts, lateral bracing, retaining, and
   site drainage. Consider a structural engineer visit (~$500–800) given the
   under-deck structure visible in p_40–p_42.
3. [ ] **Deck inspection** — ledgers, flashing, post bases, railings; multi-level
   wood decks in a wet winter / fire summer cycle are the highest-maintenance
   element on the property.
4. [ ] **Pest/WDO** — standard for hills wood construction.
5. [ ] **Roof + skylights** — pitched shingle (good), but skylights (p_11, p_12,
   p_22) are leak-maintenance items; verify age/flashing.
6. [ ] **Sewer lateral** — Oakland requires point-of-sale sewer lateral
   compliance (EBMUD PSL ordinance); in a short sale with no seller money, a
   failed lateral becomes your cost — scope it.
7. [ ] 1978 vintage: assume no asbestos/lead disturbance unless renovating;
   check electrical panel brand/capacity and any unpermitted lower-level work
   (lounge/sauna level — verify permits via Oakland permit records).

**As-is reality:** seller has no money, bank won't credit. Inspections are for
*your* go/no-go and future budget, not for negotiation. Pre-decide the walk
threshold (e.g. structural/drainage findings > $25K → walk).

## 5. Decision gates (summary)

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| 1. Lien/approval intel | ≤ 2 liens, hardship package complete, ideally pre-approved price |
| 2. Insurance | Bindable quote ≤ budget before offering |
| 3. Inspections | No structural/drainage finding > walk threshold |
| 4. Bank number | Approval at ≤ ~$1.15M |
| 5. Clock | Approval inside your deadline; else walk cleanly |

**If all gates pass:** you're getting a 3.5/5 forest house at a real discount with
eyes open on fire and process. **If any gate fails:** the bundle
(`listing.json` + this file) is the record — walk and keep hunting; the pipeline
exists precisely so walking is cheap.
