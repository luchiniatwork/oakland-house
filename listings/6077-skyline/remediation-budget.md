# Remediation Budget — 6077 Skyline Blvd, Oakland, CA 94611

What the disclosure package's own findings cost to cure, priced against 2026 Bay Area
cost data. Prepared: 2026-11-03.

Companion files: `STRATEGY.md` (anchors/gates this feeds), `disclosure-analysis.md`
(package review), `engineering-reports.md` (Bear vs Padir evidence base).

**Method & caveats.** Ranges below are budgeting numbers from published 2026 Bay Area
cost guides (see Sources), applied to the scopes documented in the package (HomeGuard
home/roof/termite 7/27/2026; Bear 1/2026; Padir 10/5/2026). They are **not bids** — every
scope needs 2–3 contractor bids during the contingency window (`STRATEGY.md` §8), early
enough to convert to credits before the clock dies. Roof area derived as ~2,000–2,300
sqft (house ~1,550 sqft footprint + ~400 sqft garage + overhangs/eaves).

### Risk reserves (NOT in the totals — price as scenarios)

- **Septic tail risk.** Certification is $0.5–1.5K, but a failed leach field on a steep
  CGS-landslide-zone parcel runs **$30–80K+** (engineered system; possibly more), and
  whether a sewer connection is even available at the Skyline frontage is unknown. Binary
  risk: resolve with the certification + sewer-availability letter BEFORE pricing, or
  demand an escrow holdback sized to the failure scenario.
- **Gate-1 retro-legalization.** If Accela shows the +271 sqft / +1 bath / 1→2-car garage
  / deck / roof / retaining-wall work unpermitted: **$20–50K+** for retro-permitting
  (plans, engineering, fees, opening finishes) — or price the legal-GLA haircut already
  baked into the ≤$1.0M anchor.

## What it does to the price math (`STRATEGY.md` §2 anchors)

| Path | Math | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Pay ask $1.25M, cure everything | $1.25M + ~$125K = **$1.375M all-in** | Dead — 18% over the $1.167M break-even |
| Buyer cures everything, hold break-even | $1.167M − $125K ≈ **$1.04M price** | Validates §8's **≤$1.0M open** on 1,280 legal sqft: $1.0M + $125K = $1.125M all-in ✓ |
| Seller cures Padir package + punch list; roof credit $40–50K; buyer carries rest | price up to **~$1.1M**; $1.1M + ~$60K = ~$1.16M all-in | Works — this is the §8 trade menu made concrete |
| Any price > $1.1M without credits | all-in > $1.2M | Walk (§7) |

**Rule of thumb:** every $10K of documented cure the seller completes properly (permits,
named contractor, photos) is $10K of price headroom; every $10K left to you is $10K off
the price — plus contingency for the seller's demonstrated cure-quality problem (their
"15 of 15 complete" did not survive their own second engineer).
