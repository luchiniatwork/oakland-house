## 3. Gate 1 — Permits & disclosures (the documentary spine)

The renovation delta (+271 sqft, +1 full bath, 1→2 car garage, rebuilt deck,
new water heater, gut kitchen/3 baths) almost entirely required permits.

- [ ] **Run Accela yourself** (aca-prod.accela.com/oakland, free, by address) —
  before offering. Do not rely on seller's word either way.
- [ ] **Demand the AB 968 disclosure** in the offer: all work itemized, copies of
  permits + finals, contractor names/contacts. Then **CSLB-verify the contractor**
  (license status, classification, complaints).
- [ ] Match scope: addition sqft, new bath, garage conversion/rebuild, deck
  rebuild, water heater, panel/electrical, plumbing. Every line needs a finaled
  permit or an explanation.
- [ ] Pull the **Feb 2026 grant deed** (Alameda Clerk-Recorder) to name the
  flipper entity; search for their other flips (pattern of unpermitted work?).
- [ ] Reconcile the **NHD statement**: must show VHFHSZ yes, landslide
  Zone-of-Required-Investigation **yes**, fault zone no. Any miss = disclosure
  problem to price or walk on.
- [ ] **EBMUD PSL Compliance Certificate** — legally required before title
  transfer; check status free at eastbaypsl.com. No certificate = seller must cure.

**Scenario responses:**

| Scenario | Response |
|---|---|
| Permits exist, all finaled | Proceed to physical gates; pricing per §2 |
| Permits pulled, never finaled | Seller reopens + finals inspections **pre-close at their cost**; no close on expired permits |
| No permits, cosmetic only claimed | Independent verification (inspector + Accela); re-anchor price to 1,280 sqft |
| No permits on addition/bath/garage | Seller retro-legalizes pre-close, OR price = legal GLA + escrow **holdback** sized to legalization bids (get 2 contractor estimates during contingency) |
| No permits on **deck/foundation** | Hardest stop — see §7 walk triggers |
| Seller stonewalls AB 968 | Treat as concealment signal; offer only on legal-GLA pricing with full inspection rights, or walk |

## 4. Gate 2 — Fire & insurance (VHFHSZ, gating item)

- [ ] **Quotes BEFORE removing any contingency** — target during inspection period,
  not after: 2 admitted carriers (expect declines) + **CA FAIR Plan + DIC wrap**
  as the real baseline. Realistic budget **$6–9K/yr** (94611: ~2,800 FAIR Plan
  homes, 5.48% nonrenewal rate, anecdotal FAIR $6K + wrap $2K).
- [ ] Ask for seller's current carrier/premium + **CLUE claims report** (flipper's
  policy history on the house).
- [ ] **Unpermitted-work insurance check:** if Gate 1 finds ANY unpermitted
  electrical/plumbing, get written carrier confirmation they will bind anyway —
  unpermitted electrical in VHFHSZ is a claim-denial vector.
- [ ] **Defensible-space bid during contingency:** eucalyptus sea below the deck,
  redwood/pine touching walls, dry slopes — arborist quote for limb-up/removal +
  annual maintenance number for the affordability model.
- [ ] Hardening scope for later credits: ember vents, gutter guards, 0–5 ft
  non-combustible zone; note roof covering is flat membrane (not Class A rated
  assembly by default — ask roofer for the assembly's fire rating).
- [ ] Context for the file: Skyline is a designated evacuation corridor with
  CAL FIRE-funded fuel work; the recognized Firewise community (Hansom–Parkridge)
  is miles south — **no Firewise credit here**; yearly OFD vegetation inspections
  apply.

## 5. Gate 3 — Physical diligence (structure, roof, systems)

Inspection budget ~$2–3.5K, sunk if gates fail. Order = cheapest/most decisive first.

1. [ ] **General home inspection** — with explicit instruction to distinguish
   permitted-era work from flip work and to probe flip tells (popcorn texture in
   bath, windowless tiled annex, staging-grade fixtures).
2. [ ] **Roof certification + drainage review** — **flat membrane roofs on BOTH
   house and garage, age and warranty unknown, with skylights** (classic
   winter-leak typology; prior roof was tar & gravel). Demand: roofer's cert with
   remaining-life opinion, membrane seams/penetrations, ponding, skylight
   flashing, scupper/drain flow test. Ask seller for roof invoice/warranty from
   the flip. **No roof cert, no contingency removal.**
3. [ ] **Structural engineer + geotech consult** (~$800–1.5K) — 12–15 ft stucco
   downslope cripple/foundation wall + cantilevered deck, inside a CGS landslide
   Zone of Required Investigation. Scope: foundation wall condition/reinforcing,
   **deck ledger attachment and flashing**, lateral bracing, slope drainage
   directed away from the downhill neighbor, any post-1961 retrofit evidence.
   This is the house's #1 life-safety item — the deck hangs over a 12–15 ft drop.
4. [ ] **Pest/WDO** — standard hills wood construction.
5. [ ] **Sewer lateral scope** — even with PSL certificate, camera it; 1961 clay
   laterals on slopes fail; PSL test is pressure, not condition.
6. [ ] **Electrical panel** — brand/capacity, any Federal Pacific/Zinsco legacy,
   AFCI/GFCI coverage in the new work; flip electrical is where fires start.
7. [ ] **Plumbing** — supply + drain materials in new baths, water heater permit
   and strapping, pressure test.
8. [ ] **Hillside drainage** — where does roof + site water go on a 0.43-ac steep
   lot with a downhill neighbor ~20–30 ft below? Gutters, downspout daylighting,
   French drains; winter saturation is the landslide trigger.

## 7. Walk-away triggers (pre-commit)

| Trigger | Why it's fatal |
|---|---|
| Deck/foundation work unpermitted AND seller won't cure or credit | Life-safety + landslide zone + uninsurable unknown — compounding, not incremental |
| Roof cert shows active leaks / <5 yr life AND no credit | Winter is coming; flat membranes + skylights |
| Insurance unbindable, or FAIR+DIC quote > budget ceiling (~$9K/yr) | Carrying cost breaks the model |
| Appraisal shortfall > cash tolerance on unpermitted-GLA basis | $831/sf doesn't survive 1,280-sqft underwriting |
| AB 968 disclosure evasive/false | Concealment pattern = trust collapse on everything else |
| Structural/geotech finding > ~$25–40K remediation without seller credit | Steep-lot structure on a flip margin |
| Final price > $1.2M | Value math dies (3.08 < Rincon 3.17, and Rincon has no structural tripwires) |

## 8. Offer sequence

**Pre-offer (free, this week):** Accela permit search → PSL status → price
verification → call agent: what killed the Sep 22 escrow? current price real?
seller's timeline pressure?

**Offer structure:**
- Price: open **$1.05–1.1M** if Gate-1 intel is clean; open **≤$1.0M anchored on
  1,280 legal sqft** if permits are missing or unanswered.
- Terms: inspection contingency ≥ 14 days; loan + appraisal contingencies intact;
  explicit **insurance-bindability contingency** (VHFHSZ standard practice);
  AB 968 disclosure + permit copies due within 3 days of acceptance.
- Ask for seller-paid: roof certification, CLUE report, PSL certificate (their
  legal obligation anyway).
- Concession menu (trade, don't give): price ↔ seller retro-permitting; price ↔
  escrow holdback (2 contractor bids for legalization + deck engineering);
  price ↔ roof/deck credits; close speed ↔ price (flipper carrying cost is their
  pain — a fast clean close is your best non-price currency).

**During contingency:** run §3–§6 in parallel; get legalization/hardening bids
early enough to convert findings into credits before the contingency clock dies.

**Before removing contingencies:** every §7 trigger checked clear; insurance
bound in writing; permits/finals in hand or priced.

## 9. Decision gates (summary)

| Gate | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| 0. Price/status | Current list price + fallen-escrow cause known |
| 1. Permits/AB 968 | Finaled permits match scope, OR repriced to legal GLA + holdback |
| 2. Insurance | Written bindable quote ≤ budget |
| 3. Structure | Engineer/geotech: no >$25–40K finding; deck ledger sound or cured |
| 4. Roof | Certification: sound, ≥5 yr life, no active leaks |
| 5. Number | Final ≤ $1.167M target, ≤ $1.2M absolute |

**If all gates pass:** a verified 4.5/5 view at defensible value with the risk
stack retired or priced. **If any gate fails:** `listing.json` +
`web-research.md` + this file are the record — walking is cheap by design.
