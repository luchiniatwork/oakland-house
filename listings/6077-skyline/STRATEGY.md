## 3. Gate 1 — Permits & disclosures (the documentary spine)

- [ ] **Demand the AB 968 disclosure** in the offer: all work itemized, copies of

- [ ] Match scope: addition sqft, new bath, garage conversion/rebuild, deck
  rebuild, water heater, panel/electrical, plumbing. Every line needs a finaled
  permit or an explanation.

- [ ] Reconcile the **NHD statement**: must show VHFHSZ yes, landslide
  Zone-of-Required-Investigation **yes**, fault zone no. Any miss = disclosure
  problem to price or walk on.
- [ ] **EBMUD PSL Compliance Certificate** — legally required before title
  transfer; check status free at eastbaypsl.com. No certificate = seller must cure.

**Scenario responses:**

status** (package says septic) → price verification → call agent: what killed the Sep 22

**Then serve a written BMI demand** (full question list: `disclosure-analysis.md` §5) for

Chi Zhen Construction (Robert Sun) and demand the foundation contractor's identity.

**Offer structure:**
- Price: open **$1.05–1.1M** if Gate-1 intel is clean; open **≤$1.0M anchored on
  1,280 legal sqft** if permits are missing or unanswered.
- Terms: inspection contingency ≥ 14 days; loan + appraisal contingencies intact;
  explicit **insurance-bindability contingency** (VHFHSZ standard practice);
  AB 968 disclosure + permit copies due within 3 days of acceptance.
- Ask for seller-paid: roof certification (or replacement credit — their own report
  says beyond repair), CLUE report, septic certification (package says septic, not
  sewer — PSL likely N/A).
- Concession menu (trade, don't give): price ↔ seller retro-permitting; price ↔
  escrow holdback (2 contractor bids for legalization + deck engineering);
  price ↔ roof/deck credits; close speed ↔ price (flipper carrying cost is their
  pain — a fast clean close is your best non-price currency).

**During contingency:** run §3–§6 in parallel; get legalization/hardening bids
early enough to convert findings into credits before the contingency clock dies.

**Before removing contingencies:** every §7 trigger checked clear; insurance
bound in writing; permits/finals in hand or priced.
