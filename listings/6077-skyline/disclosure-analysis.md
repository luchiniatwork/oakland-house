## 0. Verdict

The SPQ and TDS (both signed by Stanley Yeung 8/7/2026 and Peitong Duan 8/11/2026) contain
**at least a dozen answers that are false or materially incomplete**, each contradicted by
inspection reports, repair records, or other documents the sellers possessed **before
signing**. The pattern is systematic: every defect question answered "No," while the
same envelope/package delivers the reports proving the defects.

This is the STRATEGY §7 walk-trigger scenario — **"AB 968 disclosure evasive/false →
concealment pattern = trust collapse on everything else."** The practical posture: trust
nothing on the forms; price and gate entirely on the underlying reports and independent
verification.

The seller's approach has a double edge worth understanding: they DID deliver the reports
(TDS §I checks substituted disclosures: Home Inspection, Termite, Roof). The bad news is
in the package. But the signed forms deny it — which is their legal exposure and our
leverage.
