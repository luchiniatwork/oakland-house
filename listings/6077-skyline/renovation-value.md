- **Crawlspace retaining wall rotated 5.4°** (load-bearing; buttress before rains) +
  active drainage leaks → −$25–45K (the under-cured pre-existing condition).
- **Unpermitted conversion + bath + systems work** → −$20–50K legalization risk (or the
  legal-GLA pricing haircut in STRATEGY §2).

  None), 1,551 sqft (1,407 + conversion) → trust discount.

**Monthly burn:** ~$10–11K (opportunity cost ~$9K + insurance/tax/utilities ~$1.5K) —
**~$60K+ to reach the spring market** through the rainy season, with an unbuttressed
wall (their engineer's words: "before the rainy season") and a failed roof overhead.

**Verdict: the renovation carries value only up to ~$1.0M.** The genuine upgrades
(kitchen, baths, A/C, panels, floors) are worth roughly $200–300K over the $655K basis,
but the defective envelope work (roof, decks, retaining wall), the under-cured structure,
and the unpermitted conversion claw back $125–215K of it. At **$1.25M the seller is
pricing ~$250K of renovation value that does not exist**. Note the convergence: as-is
value (~$1.0M) ≈ their all-in cost (~$1.05M) — the market (two cuts, one fallen escrow,
59 DOM) has already figured this out. Their concealment strategy is margin defense, not
information strategy.

## 5. What this changes in the strategy

1. **The sqft lever gets sharper.** It's not "1,280 legal vs 1,551 claimed" alone — the
   pre-flip plan (their own agent's marketing) shows **1,407 sqft measured** and a
   145-sqft storage room that became the 3rd bath. Demand in writing: was the storage
   conversion permitted? Was the Sun Room ever permitted (county says 1,280)? Price on
   **1,280 county / 1,407 measured** until permits appear — and use the ~145-sqft
   unpermitted conversion as a specific credit line, not a vague threat.
2. **Add the "2-car garage" misrepresentation to the inaccuracy list.** Pre-flip plan:
   13'10"×19'0". The structure never changed. TDS "Attached ☒ / Carport ☒" also wrong.
3. **Fireplace:** demolished by the flip (chimney flue remains on the roof — include
   removal/cap in the re-roof scope; MLS "Fireplaces: 1" is stale/wrong).
4. **Offer math stands, strengthened:** open **≤$1.0M** (their all-in cost zone), hold
   break-even at $1.167M; the renovation-value analysis says even $1.0M is fair, not a
   lowball — the evidence file now proves it line by line.
5. **Inspection targeting:** the converted-storage bath + bedroom bumps sit over the
   rotated crawlspace wall zone ("the new addition shows out-of-plumb conditions" —
   Padir ch. 9). Direct the independent engineer there specifically.
