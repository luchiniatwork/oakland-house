Negotiation playbook for pursuing (or walking from) this failed-flip listing.
Companion files: `listing.json` (scored record, 3.2/5), `card.txt`,
`crosscheck.md` (marketing-vs-photo verification), `photos/`. Modeled on

No disclosure package or public-record research pulled yet (see §10).
Last updated: 2026-11-05 (initial version from bundle data + comp analysis).

**Core mental model:** this house IS the cheapest turnkey ticket in the set
plus a risk stack whose costs recur (insurance, defensible space, stilt
maintenance, resale-pool shrinkage). The ask is priced like a normal house with
a quality discount; the gates below convert risk into price. The seller's
withdrawal pattern means leverage comes from an **accepted offer**, not public
cuts — don't wait for a $639K print that may never come.

## 1. Leverage inventory (why you can push)

- **50+ DOM** in a market where every comparable-quality listing went pending
  in ≤21 days (Snake 12d, Rincon 12d, Wilton 14d, 7094 Pinehaven 14d). Balboa
  is the stale outlier alongside 6077 Skyline (59d) — the two high-$/sf,
  heavy-flag listings.
- **TWO failed prior cycles** — the market already rejected $895K and $849K.
  Price discovery is moving down and isn't done.
- **Failed-flip economics:** $962.5K basis (Feb 2023) + 2023 reno; deeply
  underwater at ask. If financed (likely flip loan — see §10), carrying costs
  and loan maturity are the pressure; year-end + winter season amplify both.
- **Sqft discrepancy:** marketed 923 vs measured 890. Negotiate against
  measured GLA and the seller's basis, never against the ask.
- **Documented marketing over-spin** (`crosscheck.md`): "light-filled"
  half-true, "views from the shower" oversold, drone bay/SF shots imply views
  the house lacks, feed tag "Waterfront" flat false. Credibility discount:
  every claim gates on documents, not the listing copy.
- **The risk stack is itself the lever:** insurance quotes, transmission
  easement, stilt engineering — each DD finding is a priced credit or a walk
  trigger.
- **AB 968 does NOT apply** (owned since Feb 2023, >18 months) — unlike
  Skyline, no statutory flip-disclosure duty. No AB 968 duty ≠ no permit duty:
  demand the 2023 permit list anyway (§3).

## 3. Gate 1 — Permits, title & disclosures (the documentary spine)

The 2023 reno scope (gut kitchen, 2 baths, roof, electrical, plumbing per
marketing) almost entirely required permits.

- [ ] **Run Accela yourself** (aca-prod.accela.com/oakland, free, by address) —
  before offering. Match scope: kitchen, baths, roof, panel/electrical,
  plumbing, water heater, deck work (if any).
- [ ] **Pull the Feb 2023 grant deed + prelim** (Alameda Clerk-Recorder /
  title co): name the owner entity (flipper LLC vs occupant — predicts
  capitulation vs withdrawal), financed vs cash (leverage intel), and map the
  **HV transmission easement**: holder, width, distance from deck/posts, any
  rights or structures **over the lot itself** (fatal — §7) vs strictly
  downslope (priceable).
- [ ] Demand permit copies + finals for the 2023 work within 3 days of
  acceptance; **CSLB-verify** any named contractor.
- [ ] Reconcile the **NHD statement**: must show VHFHSZ yes; confirm
  independently on CAL FIRE FHSZ viewer + Oakland GIS. Any miss = disclosure
  problem to price or walk on.
- [ ] **EBMUD PSL Compliance Certificate** — required before title transfer;
  check free at eastbaypsl.com. (Balboa is in-city Montclair — presumed public
  sewer, unlike Skyline's septic; verify.)
- [ ] Confirm **no HOA** (none mentioned anywhere in bundle; verify).

**Scenario responses:**

| Scenario | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Permits exist, all finaled | Proceed to physical gates; pricing per §2 |
| Permits pulled, never finaled | Seller reopens + finals inspections pre-close at their cost |
| No permits on kitchen/baths/roof/electrical/plumbing | Independent verification + price credit sized to retro-legalization bids; unpermitted electrical in VHFHSZ is a claim-denial vector (§4) |
| Transmission easement strictly downslope | Priceable: EMF/comfort discount, resale-pool shrinkage — hold §2 target |
| Transmission easement/structures over the lot | Hardest stop — see §7 walk triggers |
| Seller stonewalls | Offer only on measured-GLA pricing with full inspection rights, or walk |

## 4. Gate 2 — Fire & insurance (VHFHSZ presumptive — THE make-or-break gate)

The entire discount thesis rests on insurance being **expensive but bindable**.
If quotes come back extreme or unavailable, the price model doesn't just
worsen — the buyer pool collapses toward all-cash investors (§2 floor).

- [ ] **Quotes BEFORE removing any contingency** — during inspection period:
  2 admitted carriers (expect declines) + **CA FAIR Plan + DIC wrap** as the
  real baseline. Realistic budget **$6–9K/yr** (94611: ~2,800 FAIR Plan homes,
  5.48% nonrenewal rate — same zip as Skyline).
- [ ] Ask for seller's current carrier/premium + **CLUE claims report**.
- [ ] **Stilt + wood-deck + VHFHSZ carrier check:** elevated combustible
  construction over slope fuels narrows appetite further — get written
  confirmation they will bind the structure as-built.
- [ ] **Unpermitted-work check:** if Gate 1 finds ANY unpermitted
  electrical/plumbing, written carrier confirmation they bind anyway.
- [ ] **Defensible-space bid during contingency:** eucalyptus canopy over the
  roof + litter at foundation — arborist quote for limb-up/removal plus an
  annual maintenance number for the affordability model.
- [ ] Hardening credits (later): ember vents, gutter guards, 0–5 ft
  non-combustible zone. Positive vs Skyline: pitched comp-shingle roof, likely
  Class A assembly — confirm with roofer.
- [ ] Context for the file: yearly OFD vegetation inspections apply in the
  hills; check for any prior violations on the parcel.

## 5. Gate 3 — Physical diligence (structure first, systems verify)

Inspection budget ~$2–3K, sunk if gates fail. Order = most decisive first.

1. [ ] **Structural engineer** (~$800–1.5K) — **the house's #1 item**: tall
   braced timber posts carrying the downhill half + cantilevered deck
   (p_36–37). Scope: post condition/connections, diagonal bracing adequacy,
   **deck ledger attachment and flashing**, foundation drainage, any retrofit
   evidence. Steep-lot stilt construction + fire zone = compounding risk.
2. [ ] **General home inspection** — explicit instruction to distinguish
   original-1959 work from 2023 flip work; probe flip tells (staging-grade
   fixtures, surface-over-substance finishes).
3. [ ] **Roof verification (cheap):** new comp roof visible (p_37, p_44) —
   demand the 2023 invoice/warranty, age, transferability. No roof-doc, no
   contingency removal — but far lower-risk than Skyline's flat membrane.
4. [ ] **Pest/WDO** — hills wood construction + eucalyptus debris at the
   foundation line.
5. [ ] **Sewer lateral scope** — 1959 clay laterals fail; PSL test is
   pressure, not condition.
6. [ ] **Electrical panel** — verify the 2023 rewire claim: brand/capacity,
   no Federal Pacific/Zinsco legacy, AFCI/GFCI coverage.
7. [ ] **Plumbing** — verify the 2023 repipe claim: supply + drain materials,
   water heater permit/strapping.
8. [ ] **Hillside drainage** — where does roof + site water go around the
   posts and under the deck? Winter saturation under stilts = movement.
9. [ ] **Transmission corridor site check** — stand on the deck at different
   times of day; sightline to towers; optional cheap EMF-meter reading. This
   is a comfort/pricing datum, not a fixable defect.

## 7. Walk-away triggers (pre-commit)

| Trigger | Why it's fatal |
| --- | --- |
| Insurance unbindable, or FAIR+DIC quote > budget ceiling (~$9K/yr) | Carrying cost breaks the model; buyer pool collapses |
| Transmission easement/structures over the lot itself | Unfinanceable/uninsurable unknown, not priceable |
| Stilt posts or deck finding > ~$25–40K remediation without seller credit | Compounds with fire zone; flip margin won't absorb it |
| Unpermitted electrical/plumbing in VHFHSZ AND seller won't cure/credit | Claim-denial vector in a fire zone |
| Disclosure concealment pattern (NHD miss, reno claims false) | Trust collapse on everything else |
| Seller holds > $699K (or reprices up) | 50 DOM says the clearing price is at/below ask — never chase |
| Final price > $699K with any gate merely "priced" | Risk stack must be retired or discounted, not absorbed at full ask |

## 8. Offer sequence

**Pre-offer (free, this week):** Accela permit search → PSL status →
price/status verification → Feb 2023 grant deed / owner entity / financed vs
cash → CAL FIRE + Oakland GIS VHFHSZ confirm → call agent: why did the $895K /
$849K cycles fail — no offers or rejected offers? any offers on the current
run? seller's timeline pressure? is the entity an investor?

**Offer structure:**
- Price: open **$640–670K** (data-supported, per §2); open **$615–630K** only
  with clean terms + short close if the agent confirms investor seller /
  financing pressure — the off-list lowball is the realistic path to the
  capitulation zone.
- Terms: inspection contingency ≥ 12–14 days; loan + appraisal contingencies
  intact; explicit **insurance-bindability contingency** (VHFHSZ standard
  practice); 2023 permit copies + finals due within 3 days of acceptance.
- Ask for seller-paid: CLUE report, roof warranty documentation/transfer,
  arborist/defensible-space bid (converts the eucalyptus flag into a number).
- Concession menu (trade, don't give): price ↔ defensible-space credit;
  price ↔ seller-performed structural cures with engineering sign-off;
  close speed ↔ price (investor carrying cost is their pain — a fast clean
  close is your best non-price currency).

**During contingency:** run §3–§6 in parallel; get defensible-space and any
structural-cure bids early enough to convert findings into credits before the
clock dies.

**If it sits instead:** watch for the distress tells — "price improvement"
relist language, a pending → back-on-market flip (each insurance DD cycle that
kills a deal moves the price), or "subject to lender approval" wording. The
last one is the Pinehaven precedent: sharpen the pencil below $600K.

**Before removing contingencies:** every §7 trigger checked clear; insurance
bound in writing; permits/finals in hand or priced.

record — walking is cheap by design, and the withdrawal pattern says it may
come back around anyway.
