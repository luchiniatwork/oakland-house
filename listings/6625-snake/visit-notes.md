# Visit Notes — 6625 Snake Rd, Oakland, CA 94611

Recorded 2026-11-05 from the buyers' site visit (companion records: `card.txt`,
`listing.json` 3.4/5, `crosscheck.md`). **Verdict: no joy — renovation-heavy.
Demoted to market-tell only** for the 318-capricorn strategy.

## Issues observed (buyer-reported)

1. **No real garage.** Converted into office + gym — two things we don't need.
   Only a small carport remains. The listing's headline "flex space" is space
   we wouldn't use; permit status of the conversion unknown (diligence item if
   ever revisited).
2. **Kitchen needs total redesign + relocation.** Dark, old, poorly located.
   Moving a kitchen = plumbing/gas/venting (no hood exists — ceiling fan only),
   design, permits → **$100–175K** Bay Area. Silver lining: post-and-beam
   partitions are usually non-structural, so a move is feasible.
3. **Bedroom-count fudge confirmed in person.** One "bedroom" is a small office
   behind the kitchen that would disappear into the kitchen redesign.
   Post-renovation functional count ≈ 2 real beds + downstairs bonus ≈ **2.5bd**
   (card's "mild fudge" was generous).
4. **View blocked by tree growth.** Clearing/limbing required — the cheapest
   item on the list (**$3–15K**) and it doubles as defensible-space/insurance
   work. Caveats: Oakland protected-oak rules (limbing >> removal), regrowth is
   maintenance, and clearing may expose the deck edge to the neighbor at
   15–40 ft (p_41). Reward is a canyon view only — never water.
5. **No en-suite, and no obvious place to add one.** Bones problem: a new bath
   needs drain/stack access in a post-and-beam on stilts — **$50–80K if a path
   exists at all**. The layout is telling us it's a 2-bath house.
6. **Downstairs second bath needs renovation** (**$30–60K**; card already
   flagged budget-grade, p_35).

## Renovation math (our scope)

- Core (kitchen move + downstairs bath + trees): **~$135–250K**
- Extended (en-suite, garage reconversion, flat membrane roofs ×2, decks/
  retaining wall): +$125–200K more
- **All-in at ask: $985K–1.10M** for a still-2-bath, carport, stilt-supported,
  Very High FHSZ house (~$650–700/sf effective) — worse than 318 Capricorn
  all-in ($945–995K, move-in polished, 1,655 sqft, kitchen already redone).
  Paying retail for the house AND retail for the renovation; the $100K-under-
  2022 ask is the market already pricing condition, and it still doesn't pencil.
